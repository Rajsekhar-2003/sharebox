About ShareBox

ShareBox is a fast, secure, and user-friendly sharing platform built with Python (Flask) and Bootstrap. Started in 2024, it now supports over 20,000+ trusted users worldwide. ShareBox lets you share text or files instantly—no phone number, no account, no hassle.

Our goal is simple: make sharing instant, private, and effortless. Whether you're sending notes, code snippets, documents, or images — ShareBox creates a secure, fast-access link that you can share anywhere.

Why ShareBox?

  • Fast: Create & share in seconds.
  • Secure: Auto-expiring links protect your data.
  • Easy: No login, no setup required.
  • No phone number needed: Share with a short code.
  • 20k+ users trust ShareBox for daily sharing.
  • QR Code support: Perfect for mobile access.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make global sharing seamless. ShareBox helps people connect instantly, safely, and effortlessly worldwide—breaking communication barriers.
